## About Me

I am an analyst, investor, and writer focused on **financial modeling, company research, valuation, and financial statement analysis**.

I build Excel-based financial models using public company filings, historical financial statements, investor materials, and independent business research.

My work is centered on understanding how a business actually operates — what drives revenue, margins, cash flow, returns on capital, and ultimately valuation.

The objective is not simply to build spreadsheets. It is to use financial modeling and research to understand a business, identify the assumptions that matter, evaluate potential outcomes, and make better-informed decisions.

### Modeling Process

My typical research and modeling process includes:

1. Reviewing company filings, earnings materials, and historical financial statements
2. Researching the company's products, customers, competitors, and industry
3. Identifying the primary drivers of revenue, margins, cash flow, and returns on capital
4. Reconstructing historical financial performance in Excel
5. Developing operating assumptions and financial forecasts
6. Analyzing capital structure, debt, and cash flow
7. Building multiple valuation scenarios
8. Identifying the assumptions, risks, catalysts, and expectations that matter most
